Simple rules for great journeys.

These terms outline the general conditions that apply when you book tours, activities, transportation or other travel services with Max Tours & Safari.

ON THIS PAGE
01

Booking & Payment

A booking becomes confirmed once the required deposit or payment has been received and the travel arrangements have been accepted by our team.

  • Advance booking is recommended, especially during peak seasons.
  • A deposit may be required depending on the selected package.
  • The remaining balance must be paid according to the agreed booking schedule.
  • Payment instructions will be provided during the booking process.
  • Confirmation will be sent once the booking arrangements are secured.
02

Cancellation & Changes

Cancellation and amendment conditions may vary depending on the tour, accommodation and third-party services involved.

  • Cancellation requests should be communicated as early as possible.
  • Refund eligibility depends on the timing and conditions of the booking.
  • Third-party cancellation charges may apply.
  • Changes to confirmed bookings may be subject to availability.
  • Force majeure situations may require rescheduling or alternative arrangements.
03

Travel Information

Guests are responsible for ensuring that they have the documents required for their journey.

  • A valid passport and required travel documents should be carried.
  • Guests should check applicable visa and entry requirements before travelling.
  • Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
  • Guests should provide accurate information when making a booking.
04

Health & Safety

Your safety is important to us. Some activities involve outdoor, marine or physical environments.

  • Guests should follow instructions provided by guides and activity operators.
  • Guests should inform us in advance about relevant allergies or health considerations.
  • Swimming ability may be required for certain water activities.
  • Activities may be modified when conditions are considered unsafe.
05

Itinerary Changes

Travel conditions can occasionally change because of weather, availability, operational requirements or other circumstances.

  • Minor itinerary adjustments may be necessary.
  • Weather-sensitive activities may be rescheduled.
  • Alternative arrangements may be offered when practical.
  • We will make reasonable efforts to communicate important changes.
