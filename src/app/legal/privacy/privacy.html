01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Booking and travel preferences.
  • Messages or inquiries you send to us.
  • Information voluntarily provided through booking forms.
02

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to provide and improve our travel services, including:

  • Processing and managing bookings.
  • Responding to questions and inquiries.
  • Communicating important travel information.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Improving our website and services.
03

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, certain information may need to be shared with trusted service providers when required to arrange your trip.

For example, relevant booking information may be provided to accommodation providers, transport operators or activity providers where necessary to deliver the service you requested.

04

Data Protection

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

We aim to keep your information secure throughout our relationship with you.
05

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including booking, accounting, customer support and legal requirements.

06

Your Choices & Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information.

You may also contact us if you have concerns about how your information has been handled.

07

Questions About Privacy?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the information we hold about you, please contact our team.
